1. The Reference Hunter
“Before you design anything, find 10 real websites in my niche that look great. Screenshot them and explain what makes each one work: layout, typography, colours and spacing. Save the findings as DESIGN.md so every page follows a consistent design language.”
2. The Three Mockups
“Create three completely different mockups on one page, each inspired by a different reference, so I can compare and select one. Don't begin development until I approve a design.”
3. The AI-Look Ban
“No Inter font, no purple gradients, no glowing buttons, no serif headlines with one word in italics, and no four statistic cards in a row. If it looks like every other AI-generated website, start over.”
4. The Stranger Judge
Paste this prompt into a new chat along with a screenshot of the website.

“You've never seen this website before. Score it from 1 to 10 against three websites I admire. Identify everything that looks AI-generated, starting with the worst elements. Be critical and objective. Then recommend three changes that would bring the design to a 9/10.”
5. The Polish Pass
“Use shadcn/ui for every component and Magic UI for subtle animated details. Check every page at 375px screen width and refine the spacing, alignment, responsiveness and hover states before finalising the website.”
