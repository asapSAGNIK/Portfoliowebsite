# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Early-stage startup founders, founding engineers, and technical hiring managers evaluating Sagnik Chowdhury for founding or product engineer roles. They spend 30–60 seconds scanning a candidate's portfolio to judge engineering velocity, taste, shipping judgment, and whether they would want to build side-by-side with him.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Sagnik Chowdhury (Kolkata, India) that proves he ships end-to-end production systems (AI pipelines, automation, full-stack web and mobile products) with strong design taste and clarity. Success means a founder reads the single-scroll home page, immediately trusts his engineering and product sensibility, explores a project or his web resume, and emails him (`sagnikwork20@gmail.com`) or opens his GitHub/LinkedIn.

## Positioning

Unlike generic developer templates overloaded with particles, neon glows, or vanity stats, Sagnik's site leads with real shipped systems (10,000+ jobs indexed across 100+ companies at Uphook.ai, Gemini Vision + Twilio/Deepgram/ElevenLabs automation at Daga Groups, solo end-to-end e-commerce at Sunder Garments, plus autonomous AI tools like The Teatime, Smart Playlist, P.L.A.T.E, Sleepify, and Rocket Adventures 3D) paired with authentic personality—house music, DJing, gaming, and sports—expressed through one restrained, muted-by-default sound deck rather than gimmicks.

## Operating Context

- Three routes: `/` (single-scroll, text-heavy narrative home), `/projects` (deeper engineering archive with architecture, stack, and live/GitHub links), and `/resume` (clean printable HTML resume with one-click download for `/resume.pdf`).
- Visitors arrive from cold emails, X/LinkedIn DMs, GitHub profiles, and job applications on both desktop and mobile (must hold cleanly down to 375px).
- Sound is strictly opt-in (muted by default); no autoplay and no uninvited hover audio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Next.js 15 / React 19 / Tailwind CSS 3 / shadcn/ui codebase (`app/` router).
- Preserve all real links, contact details, resume PDF (`/resume.pdf`), portrait (`/prf@3x.jpg`), project metadata, and factual claims from `app/page.tsx` and `public/resume.pdf`.
- Zero invented metrics, testimonials, logos, or claims.
- Strict ban on AI-generated visual clichés: no Inter font, no purple gradients, no glowing buttons, no serif headlines with a single italicized word, no four statistic cards in a row.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof in 45 seconds:** A startup founder should grasp who Sagnik is, what he has shipped in production, and how to reach him without digging through hidden UI or animations.
2. **Text-first clarity, studio restraint:** Typography, spacing, and real prose carry the page; chrome, gradients, and decorative cards stay out of the way.
3. **Personality without noise:** Music and gaming belong in one intentional, opt-in tactile sound deck—not competing background effects or auto-triggered hover sounds.
4. **Honest material:** Every role, project, link, and number traces directly to Sagnik's real work and resume.
