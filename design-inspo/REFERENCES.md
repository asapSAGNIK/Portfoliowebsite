## 1. `prarthanagarwal.com`
- **Screenshot:** `.impeccable/reference/inspo/prarthan/1440x900.png`
- **Layout:** Centered single reading column (~680px), right-aligned minimal lowercase top nav, one candid horizontal photograph leading directly into conversational first-person prose.
- **Typography:** Condensed editorial serif greeting (`Hello There`) paired with an airy humanist sans for short, rhythmic paragraphs.
- **Colors:** Pale ice-mint paper ground (`#EAF6F7`), deep slate-teal ink (`#23424A`), zero accent clutter, dotted link underlines.
- **Spacing & Why It Works:** Generous vertical breathing room and zero cards. Trust comes from tone, framing, and restraint.

## 2. `joebell.studio`
- **Screenshot:** `.impeccable/reference/inspo/joebell/1440x900.png`
- **Layout:** Classic split editorial hero (natural daylight portrait on left, crisp heading + short bio + dated featured list on right) framed by hairline top and bottom utility bars.
- **Typography:** Bold grotesque display headline (`Hello there.`) paired with clean regular sans body and crisp underlined inline links.
- **Colors:** Matte carbon obsidian (`#0D0D0D`), pure off-white display text (`#F5F5F5`), muted neutral grey secondary copy (`#9E9E9E`), subtle 1px hairlines. Zero neon, zero glow.
- **Spacing & Why It Works:** Proves dark mode feels senior and timeless when you drop gradients, shadows, and particles and let portrait warmth carry the color.

## Synthesized Design Rules (AI-Look Ban + Hallmark Floor)
- **Typography:** No `Inter`, no `Space Grotesk`, no `Playfair`/`Fraunces`, no single italicized word inside a roman display headline, no tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels above headings.
- **Color:** No pure `#000000` + neon lime/purple glow, no purple gradients, no glowing buttons, max one intentional signal accent per palette.
- **Layout:** Single-scroll, text-heavy measure (580–720px prose), no four-stat-card strips, no card-inside-a-card nesting, real portrait photography framed with restraint.
- **Sound:** One quiet, muted-by-default sound deck integrated into the page's material language — zero autoplay, zero abrupt hover SFX.
